Indian Civil Aviation Framework

Airlines Operating in India: Operational Scope & Dividance

Civil aviation in India is categorized into three distinct operational models governed by the DGCA: Domestic Only (regional & UDAN carriers), Both Domestic & Overseas (Indian mainline carriers), and Foreign Carriers (strictly international under Indian cabotage laws).

1. Domestic Regional Only 2. Domestic & International 3. Foreign Carriers (Cabotage)
⚖️

Indian Aviation Cabotage Regulation (DGCA Law)

Under Indian civil aviation regulations and international bilateral air service agreements (Cabotage Laws), foreign airlines are strictly prohibited from carrying passengers between any two domestic Indian airports. Foreign carriers may only operate flights connecting international origin/destination airports to recognized Indian international gateways (or vice versa). Only Indian-registered air carriers (Categories 1 & 2) are licensed to operate point-to-point flights within India.
